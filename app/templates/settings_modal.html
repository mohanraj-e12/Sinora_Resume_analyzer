Frequently Asked Questions

How is the ATS Score calculated?

Sinora AI combines keyword frequency matching, skill hierarchy mapping, minimum experience verification, and semantic AI alignment using Gemini 2.5/3.6 models.

Can I create custom scoring templates for specific job roles?

Yes! Use the Skill Criteria option in the dashboard to set role-specific required skills, degree minimums, and custom prompt requirements.

Is candidate resume data confidential?

All parsed resumes and candidate profile records are stored with user-bound row level isolation and can be wiped at any time from the Privacy tab.
